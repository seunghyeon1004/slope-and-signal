Data: NASA POWER (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources) monthly API, NASA Langley Research Center. Meteorology from the MERRA-2 reanalysis (NASA GMAO); sunlight and cloud from GEWEX SRB and CERES SYN1deg. Land outlines: Natural Earth (public domain). This is an independent student project, not an official NASA product.

Use of AI: the code and text of this site were written with Claude (Anthropic) under the direction of team Slope and Signal. All numbers come from live NASA data and the project's own statistics code (written with Claude, not a statistics library), tested against scipy and pymannkendall. No independent human review of the statistics has been done.

Team Slope and Signal (Seunghyeon Baek, Kim Mi Yeon) for NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, challenge "Be An Earth System Trend Detective!". Source code (MIT).
